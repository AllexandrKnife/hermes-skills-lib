## Theoretical Model Impact

The finding that Russia systematically targets Ukrainian АЗС ("поставлена на поток") disproves the theoretical model's assumption that "АЗС should be ignored (too many, too little volume)." The actual targeting logic:

- АЗС are targeted as nodes in the military fuel logistics network (objectively used to supply AFU vehicles)
- The goal is operational-level disruption of fuel supply to Ukrainian forces, not civilian terror per se
- This is a symmetric strategy: Ukraine attacks Russian refineries (production), Russia attacks Ukrainian АЗС (distribution)
- Both sides target the opposite end of the fuel supply chain based on their capabilities

This changes the original energy-warfare model: АЗС are NOT automatically ignorable. Whether they are targeted depends on:
- Which side is doing the targeting (Russia does, Ukraine doesn't)
- The stated rationale (military logistics vs civilian deprivation)
- Available strike capabilities (drones vs missiles vs artillery)
