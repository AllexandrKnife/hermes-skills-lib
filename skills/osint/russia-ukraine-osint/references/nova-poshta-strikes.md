## Summary Statistics

- **Total strikes recorded:** 23+ distinct incidents
- **Total killed:** 13+ people (6 in 2023, 2 in 2024, 1 in 2025, 4 in 2026)
- **Total wounded:** 30+ people
- **Geographic spread:** Kharkiv (most hit, 7+), Odesa (5+), Donetsk Oblast (4+), Kyiv (2), Dnipro (2), Chernihiv Oblast (1)
- **Weapons used:** Ballistic missiles, Shahed-136/Geran-2 drones, UAVs, aviation bombs
- **Parcels destroyed:** Over 1,121 documented (likely much higher due to unreported losses)
