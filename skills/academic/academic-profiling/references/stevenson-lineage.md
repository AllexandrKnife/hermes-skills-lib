## The Researcher

**Ian Pretyman Stevenson** (1918-2007)
- Canadian-American biochemist and psychiatrist
- Founded the Division of Perceptual Studies (DOPS) at UVA School of Medicine
- Collected ~3,000 cases of children claiming past-life memories
- Known works: *Twenty Cases Suggestive of Reincarnation* (1974), *Reincarnation and Biology* (1997), *European Cases of the Reincarnation Type* (2003)

## Direct Successors / Collaborators

### Jim B. Tucker, M.D. — ALIVE
- Bonner-Lowry Professor Emeritus, UVA DOPS
- Took over Stevenson's case collection upon his retirement in 2002
- Author: *Life Before Life: A Scientific Investigation of Children's Memories of Previous Lives* (2005)
- Contact: via dops@virginia.edu

### Satwant Pasricha — ALIVE
- Former head of Clinical Psychology, NIMHANS (Bangalore, India)
- Collaborated with Stevenson since the 1970s
- ~500 Indian reincarnation cases investigated
- Co-author: *Making Sense of Near-Death Experiences* (2011)

### Erlendur Haraldsson (1931-2020) — DECEASED
- Professor of Psychology, University of Iceland
- PhD under Hans Bender (Freiburg)
- Field research: Sri Lanka, Lebanon, India
- Also studied Sathya Sai Baba phenomena and deathbed visions

### Antonia (Tonia) Mills — ALIVE
- Professor Emeritus, University of Northern British Columbia (anthropology)
- Met Stevenson in 1984, continued research on reincarnation beliefs
- Editor: *Amerindian Rebirth: Reincarnation Belief Among North American Indians and Inuit* (1994)
- Contact: via UNBC website
