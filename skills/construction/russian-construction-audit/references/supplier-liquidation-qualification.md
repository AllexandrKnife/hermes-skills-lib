# Supplier Liquidation — kompleksnaya kvalifikatsiya

## When the manufacturer/supplier was liquidated before the document date

### Scenario
Estimate says EGGER H1145 ST10 door. Actual manufacturer: OOO "RUMDORS" (liquidated 16.01.2024).
Passport date: 09.06.2026 (2 years 5 months AFTER liquidation).

### Key questions
- Is it a manufacture date or sale date? If no label — assumed manufacture date.
- Is the OTK stamp dated? If undated — weaker evidence.
- Can the contractor provide invoices showing the actual supplier?

### Legal qualification

#### Criminal (if intent proven)
- Art. 327.1 CC RF — forgery of official document (passport). Up to 2 years imprisonment.
- Art. 327.3 CC RF — use of knowingly forged document. Up to 80 000 RUB fine.
- Art. 159.2 CC RF — fraud (misrepresentation of manufacturer). Up to 300 000 RUB / 5 years.

#### Administrative
- Art. 14.7 CAO RF — consumer deception. Up to 500 000 RUB.
- Art. 14.45 CAO RF — sale without mandatory certification. Up to 300 000 RUB.

#### Civil (SC W-1 + CC RF)
- Clause 9.1.3 SC W-1 — right to unilateral termination for material non-conformance
- Clause 5.3 SC W-1 — obligation to provide certificate; passport from liquidated entity is not valid
- Art. 431.2 CC RF — 10% fine of contract price for false assurances (calculation: 64k-757k RUB)
- Art. 469, 475 CC RF — inadequate quality goods → proportionate price reduction

### Document request (prove intent)
Via contract manager, 5 business days, basis cl. 1.3.5 TZ + cl. 5.3 SC W-1:
1. Invoices/UPD on disputed materials — shows actual supplier
2. Certificate of conformity — if from liquidated entity = invalid
3. Supply contract or invoice — if dated after liquidation = fake
4. Product passport copy — cross-check with provided version

Sanction for non-provision: rejection of KS-2 / unilateral price reduction / transfer to law enforcement (art. 144-145 CPC RF).
